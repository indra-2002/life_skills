# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

Grit means staying committed to a long-term goal and continuing to work hard even when things become difficult. Success depends not only on talent, but also on passion and perseverance.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

A growth mindset means believing that skills and intelligence can improve through effort, learning, and practice. Instead of avoiding difficulties, we should see them as opportunities to grow.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

- Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions can influence our results.
- Key point: Focus on what you can control instead of blaming outside factors.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

- Put effort into learning.
- Learn from mistakes.
- Use feedback.
- Focus on progress, not just results.
- Keep trying when things become difficult.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 5

- Practice coding every day, even when a topic feels difficult.
- Treat mistakes as learning opportunities.
- Learn from people who are better than me.
- Track my progress instead of comparing myself with others.
- Stay consistent and keep trying after failures.
