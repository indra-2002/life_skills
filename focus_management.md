# Focus Management

## Question 1: What is Deep Work?

Deep work means working on an important task with full concentration without any distractions. It helps us learn difficult things, improve our skills and complete work better.

## Question 2: According to the author, how can we do deep work properly?

1. Choose a fixed time and place for focused work.
2. Keep away from phones and other distractions.
3. Work on one task at a time.
4. Avoid unnecessary social media and messages.
5. Take proper breaks after focused work.
6. Follow a regular routine.

## Question 3: How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?

I can keep a fixed time for studying or coding every day. I can keep my phone away while working and focus on one task at a time. I can take short breaks without using social media. I can also make a list of important tasks and complete them one by one.

## Question 4: What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

Social media can waste a lot of time and reduce concentration. It can cause procrastination and disturb sleep. Comparing ourselves with others can also create stress and dissatisfaction. Too much social media can reduce productivity.

## Diffused Mode

I can give my brain some rest by doing activities like meditation, walking without my phone, sitting quietly, doing Shavasana, or doing simple household work. These activities help me relax after focused work.

## Getting Started on Difficult Tasks

When I feel afraid or bored about a task, I can start with a very small step.

1. Work on the task for 30 seconds.
2. Take a short break.
3. Remind myself that I can do it.
4. Repeat and slowly increase the working time.

Starting with a small step makes the task feel easier and helps me build confidence.
