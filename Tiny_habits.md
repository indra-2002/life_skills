# Tiny Habits

## What was the most interesting thing for me

The most interesting thing for me was that small habits can make a big change. We don't have to change everything at once. We can start with a very small thing and slowly improve.

## How can I use B = MAP to make new habits easier

B = MAP means:

- M – Motivation: Why we want to do something.
- A – Ability: How easy it is to do.
- P – Prompt: A reminder to do it.

We can make a new habit easier by starting small and having a reminder. For example, after dinner, I can read one page of a book.

## Why is it important to Shine or Celebrate after completing a habit

Shining or celebrating is important because it makes us feel good after doing the habit. Even a simple "Good job" to ourselves can help us continue the habit.

## What was the most interesting idea from the 1% Better Every Day video

The most interesting idea for me was getting 1% better every day. Even a small improvement every day can give a big result after some time. It taught me that doing something regularly is better than trying to do everything in one day.

## What is the book's perspective about Identity

The book says we should focus on who we want to become, not only on our goals. For example, instead of saying "I want to read more," I can say "I am a person who reads every day." Doing the habit regularly helps build that identity.

## How can we make a habit easier to do

We can make a habit easier by making it simple.

- Keep the things ready.
- Start with a small step.
- Remove unnecessary steps.
- Keep the things we need nearby.

For example, if I want to read, I can keep my book near my bed and read one page.

## How can we make a habit harder to do

We can make a bad habit harder by making it difficult to do.

- Keep the phone away.
- Turn off notifications.
- Remove distracting apps from the home screen.
- Log out of social media.

This gives us more time to stop and think before doing the bad habit.

## What habit would I like to do more often

One habit I want to do more is reading every day. I can keep a book near me and read for 10 minutes every day. I can read after dinner so that I remember it easily. I can also keep my phone away while reading. After reading, I can feel happy that I completed it.

## What habit would I like to eliminate or do less often

One habit I want to reduce is using my phone too much. I can turn off notifications and keep my phone away while studying. I can also set a time limit for social media. When I feel like using my phone, I can try reading or going for a walk instead.
